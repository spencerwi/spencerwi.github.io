Cursed Knowledge

The following annoying/cursed facts are true (or were true at the time I learned them), and are often the product of having learned them the hard way. I'm writing them down so you, the reader, might be spared.

  • Learned on 2026-10-20: URLs are partly-case-insensitive, and partly-case-sensitive (per RFC 3986)
    The protocol (e.g. https://) and the host (e.g. www.spencerwi.com) are case-insensitive, but the entire path (e.g. /cursed-knowledge.html) is case-sensitive.
    This means that...
    • ...https://spencerwi.com/subdir/test.html and HTTPS://SPENCERWI.COM/subdir/test.html are the same URL
    • ...https://spencerwi.com/Subdir/test.html and https://spencerwi.com/subdir/test.html are different URLs
    • ...https://spencerwi.com/subdir/test.html and https://spencerwi.com/subdir/Test.html are different URLs
    • ...https://spencerwi.com/subdir/test.html?foo=bar and https://spencerwi.com/subdir/test.html?Foo=bar are different URLs
